About the lab

Software you can trust, built with AI you can trust

SODA Nexus is a research lab in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Alberta, focused on making software more reliable through the combination of program analysis and modern AI.

Our mission

Modern software systems are large, fast-changing, and increasingly hard to verify by hand. SODA Nexus exists to close that gap — building tools and techniques that help engineers find, understand, and fix defects faster, with less manual effort.

We treat large language models as a new kind of instrument for software engineering research: powerful, but only trustworthy when paired with rigorous program analysis and careful evaluation on real systems.

Our work spans automated debugging, fault localization, test generation, mining software repositories, and intelligent software operations — always grounded in problems that practicing developers actually face.

Lab history

2023

SODA Nexus is established within the ECE department to pursue AI-assisted software engineering research.

First MSc and PhD students join, launching work on automated fault localization and repository mining.
